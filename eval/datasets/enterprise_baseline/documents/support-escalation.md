# Support Escalation

## Severity levels

A severity one incident is a full outage or confirmed data loss; the response time is 15 minutes and coverage is 24/7. Severity two is a major feature degraded for many customers, with a one hour response. Severity three is a minor issue handled during business hours.

## Escalation path

Page the on-call engineer through the incident tool. If there is no acknowledgement after 15 minutes, escalate to the engineering manager; after 30 minutes, page the director of engineering. The incident commander owns communication and never debugs alone.

## Customer communication

Post an initial status page update within 30 minutes and update it every 30 minutes until resolution. Support uses the incident channel for approved wording. Never speculate about root cause or share customer data in the status update.

## Postmortems

Hold a blameless postmortem within five business days for every severity one or two incident. Record the timeline, contributing factors and follow-up actions with owners. Follow-up actions are tracked to completion in the reliability backlog.
