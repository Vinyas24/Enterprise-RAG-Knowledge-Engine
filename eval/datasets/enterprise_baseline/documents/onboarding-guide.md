# Onboarding Guide

## Before day one

IT provisions Okta, Slack, GitHub, Jira and VPN accounts before the start date. The hiring manager confirms the laptop shipping address and assigns a buddy from the same team. New hires receive a welcome email with their first-day schedule and a link to the security training.

## First day

Meet your buddy at the morning stand-up, complete security awareness training, and enroll a hardware security key in Okta. Set up the development environment with the onboarding script and join the team channel. The goal is a working laptop and a merged documentation fix by the end of the day.

## First week

Read the architecture overview, shadow a support rotation, and ship a small change through the release process. Schedule introductory chats with product, support and security. Your manager reviews the 30-day plan at the end of the week.

## Equipment

Remote employees receive a MacBook Pro, an external monitor, a keyboard and a YubiKey. Request additional equipment through the IT portal; approvals above one thousand dollars require the department head.
