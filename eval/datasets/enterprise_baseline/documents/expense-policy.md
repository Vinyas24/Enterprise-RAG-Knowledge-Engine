# Expense Policy

## Travel

Book economy travel through the corporate travel portal. The hotel cap is 250 dollars per night in major cities and 180 dollars elsewhere. Keep itemised receipts and submit them within 30 days of the trip. Personal upgrades are not reimbursable.

## Meals

The meal per diem is 75 dollars per day, including tax and tip. Alcohol is reimbursable only when a manager approves client entertainment in advance. Team meals require a list of attendees and a business purpose.

## Approval thresholds

A manager approves expenses under 1000 dollars. The finance director approves expenses from 1000 to 5000 dollars, and the chief financial officer approves anything above 5000 dollars. Split expenses across approvers are not allowed.

## Corporate cards

Use the corporate card for travel and approved software. Never store the card in a personal account. Report a lost card to the bank immediately and notify finance. Receipts for card transactions are due within 30 days.
