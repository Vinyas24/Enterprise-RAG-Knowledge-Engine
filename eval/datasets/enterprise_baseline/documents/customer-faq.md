# Customer FAQ

## Pricing

The monthly plan is billed per seat with no commitment. Annual billing includes a 20 percent discount and is invoiced upfront. Usage above the included API quota is billed monthly at the overage rate published on the pricing page.

## Billing

Invoices are due net 30. Customers may dispute an invoice within 60 days of the invoice date by contacting billing support. Late payments pause non-production access after a 14 day grace period; production access is never suspended without notice.

## Cancellation

Cancel from the billing dashboard or by contacting support. Access continues until the end of the paid term. We do not refund partial months, but unused annual credit may be applied to a future subscription.

## Data export

Customers can export documents and metadata as JSON or CSV through the export API. After cancellation, data remains available for export for 30 days and is then deleted according to the retention policy. Contact support if you need a copy of a deleted workspace.
