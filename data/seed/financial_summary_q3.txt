CONFIDENTIAL FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE SUMMARY - Q3
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL

1. Executive Summary
In Q3, Acme Robotics achieved record consolidated quarterly revenue of $42.5 million, representing a 34% year-over-year increase compared to Q3 of the prior fiscal year. Operating margin expanded to 28.2%, driven by manufacturing efficiencies at our Frankfurt assembly hub.

2. Segment Breakdown
- Enterprise Logistics AMRs: $28.1 million (66% of total revenue)
- High-Precision Stacker Systems: $10.8 million (25% of total revenue)
- Maintenance and Cloud Fleet Subscriptions: $3.6 million (9% of total revenue)

3. Confidential Strategic Initiatives
The executive committee has reserved $15 million in capital reserves for the prospective acquisition of Apex Sensor Systems, scheduled for review in Q4.
